Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Orbit Social Studio is a locally operated social media management application designed to help users connect and manage social media accounts and prepare content for publication.

1. Information We Process

When users authorize social media connections, the application may process account identifiers, profile information, authentication tokens, and content-related information, depending on the permissions granted.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate connected accounts, manage authorized integrations, prepare content, and perform publishing operations requested by users.

3. Local Storage

Orbit Social Studio operates locally on the user's computer. Local operation does not necessarily mean that all information remains on the device. Authorized requests may communicate with third-party platform APIs.

4. Third-Party Services

Orbit Social Studio may interact with TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook through their authorized interfaces. These platforms process information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Data Sharing

Information necessary to complete user-authorized operations may be transmitted to connected social media platforms. Any additional disclosures must be described in this policy.

6. Data Security

Users should protect access to their computers and connected accounts. Authentication credentials and tokens should be stored securely and must not be exposed publicly.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Users may disconnect authorized integrations and request information about deletion of locally stored application data through the application's support channel. Revoking platform access may also require visiting the platform's account settings.

8. User Rights

Users may request information about personal data processed by the application and exercise applicable privacy rights, including access, correction, and deletion.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the application or applicable requirements.

10. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the Orbit Social Studio operator using the official support contact provided with the application.
